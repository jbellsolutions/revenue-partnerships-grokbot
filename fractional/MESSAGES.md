# The fractional messages

The core email on this bot. Dream 100, Dream 500 and cold leads all carry it. Every one is a draft until Justin says yes to the words.

## Justin's words, 2026-11-20

> "I want a plain look, real person, not a bot. With all the craziness and uncertainty, we've decided to close our agency. I'm looking to work with a few companies as a staff member, a fractional staff member. Bring my secrets of AI automation and everything else to help you land partnerships, collaborations, sponsorships, speaking engagements, and podcasts. Generate leads without you having to pay anything, sharing all the industry secrets along the way. Not a marketing gimmick. Really looking for a fractional job here, and not going to work with everybody."

> "Maybe something about them, specific about them, and the reason why I reached out."

("Plain look" is how "pay look" in the dictation was read.)

## How every one of these looks

- Plain text from Justin's own mailbox, first person, signed "Justin". No images, no tracking, no logo, no signature block, no link. The ask is a reply.
- It opens with one true, specific thing about them and why he reached out. That line comes from their dossier, their list row, or the post or job ad that put them on the list. With nothing true to say, the person waits; nobody gets a made-up line.
- No figure. If they ask what it costs, see Replies.

- `{{first_name}}, a different kind of email`

>
> If that's worth a conversation, just reply here.
>
> Justin

## 2. Dream 100: written by hand, one at a time

No template. Each is written from the person's dossier and keeps these points in this order:

1. One specific thing about them or their company, and why Justin reached out to them in particular.
2. The agency is closed, and he is looking to work with a few companies as a fractional staff member.
3. What he brings: AI automation, and partnerships, collaborations, sponsorships, speaking engagements and podcasts, which are leads they don't pay for.
4. Not a marketing gimmick; a real fractional job; not working with everybody.
5. The ask: reply.

A fact the dossier has no source for is written `[to confirm]`.

## 3. Short version (a DM, where a list row has no email)

Template: `gtm-box/templates/fractional_dm.txt`. LinkedIn goes through OutFlo or Justin's own computer, never the bot's.

> Hi {{first_name}}, it's Justin. {{first_line}} We've decided to close our agency, and I'm looking to work with a few companies as a fractional staff member: I bring the AI automation, and I go get you partnerships, collaborations, sponsorships, speaking spots and podcasts. Not a gimmick, a real fractional job. Worth a quick talk?

## 4. One follow-up, a week of silence later

Template: `gtm-box/templates/fractional_follow_up.txt`. Same thread, no new subject. These words are the build's, on his points; he approves or changes them.

> Hi {{first_name}}, floating this back up. I'm looking to join a few companies as a fractional staff member and help land partnerships, sponsorships, speaking spots and podcasts. If the timing's off, no problem. If it's worth a conversation, just reply.
>
> Justin

## 5. When the reason is a job post

Lead Radar hands over companies that posted for a virtual assistant or an AI role. For those, `{{first_line}}` names the post: what they are hiring for and where it was seen. Nothing else about the post is assumed.

## Replies

The sales agent answers, after reading the person's note.

| They say | What happens |
|---|---|
| Yes, let's talk | A time on Justin's calendar, the same day |
| How does it work? | Fractional, as a member of their team, with his own tools: a low base and a commission (his words, 2026-11-20, from the reconnect messages). No figure |
| What's the base? What's the commission? | To Justin. No figure is on file |
| Is this a real person? | To Justin, no draft |
| Not now, or no | Marked, never asked again |
| Anything upset | To Justin, top of the queue |
