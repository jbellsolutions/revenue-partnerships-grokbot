# YouTube channel

Justin, 2026-11-12: "I want to build the YouTube and X AI channel." His own accounts ("My own accounts", 2026-11-12).

Justin is on camera; the bot does everything around the camera.

## Each week

1. **Topics (Monday and Thursday run).** Pick two from `data/aeo/prompts.md`, Skool and Lead Radar asks, and YouTube search suggestions for owners' AI questions. A topic is a real question with search demand, not a trend.
2. **The brief, per video** in `data/channels/youtube/<slug>/brief.md`: three title options (the question in the buyer's words), the 40 to 60 word answer he says in the first 30 seconds, an outline, the screen recording or demo to show, a thumbnail idea, and the call to action (the free 30-minute call at truerevenuepartnerships.com).
3. **After he records** (he drops the file or link in `requests/channels/`): the transcript, chapters, a description that opens with the direct answer and links the site, five tags, three to five Shorts cut points with their captions, and a Medium version handed to content-seo.
4. **Upload** through YouTube Studio in his signed-in session as **private or unlisted**, with everything filled in. He publishes, or marks `youtube_publish` auto.
5. **Repurpose:** each video becomes an X thread draft (x-channel) and a citation row in `data/aeo/citations.md`.

## Gates

- Nothing goes public without Justin's yes; the first public video waits for him in any case.
- Never comment from his account, never use bought views, subs or growth tools. Stop on any copyright claim or channel warning and tell him.
- Clients described, never named, unless they said yes on the recording.

## Weekly read

Views, watch time, click-through rate, subscribers, and comments that are leads (to sales-crm). `data/channels/youtube-week-<date>.md`: what to make more of.

## What counts

Published only with the video's public link. Booked only from GoHighLevel.
