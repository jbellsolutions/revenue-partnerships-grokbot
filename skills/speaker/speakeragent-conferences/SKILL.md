# Conference and paid-speaking research

Use the persona, dates, geography and compensation policy from the mission. Default to the Conference Alpha process below; use the historical weighted scorer only when a comparable legacy ranking is explicitly useful. Read [algorithm distinctions](../speakeragent/references/algorithms.md) and [conference playbook](references/playbook.md).

1. Generate queries from substantive topics, industries and audience problems. Spread them across conference, convention, summit, symposium, forum, workshop and congress. Use current and next year, CFPs, proposals and speaker applications. Track source coverage and retrieval failures.
2. Discover through web search, official associations/organizer calendars, relevant directories, technical conference datasets and [social speaker requests](../speakeragent-social/SKILL.md). Include seminars, webinars, workshops and panels when within the brief. Directories are leads; open the official event or application source. Use optional paid APIs only within the mission's budget. Academic paper calls are not automatically paid professional speaking.
3. Confirm a real event, current edition and legitimate speaking format. Exclude product pages, attendee-only training sales, attendee tickets, job listings and sponsor-only speaking purchases from an ordinary paid-speaker shortlist. A real call for an external seminar/workshop facilitator can qualify. Preserve potentially useful alternatives separately.
4. Extract date, location, format, organizer, CFP deadline/status, eligibility, session type and compensation with URLs and short evidence excerpts. Do not treat a ticket price or sponsorship package as a speaker fee. Retain unknown dates for research; do not count them as deadline-ready.
5. Apply hard client constraints, then assess genuine topic/audience fit. A broad AI keyword is insufficient. Save a one-sentence reason using only supplied facts. A failed relevance call remains unreviewed, not approved.
6. Deduplicate by event identity and edition, preserving separate cities/dates/tracks when meaningful. Keep all discovery sources. Do not collapse an organizer's entire portfolio into one domain record.
7. Save cases before deeper research. Use [contact research](../speakeragent-contact-research/SKILL.md) for relevant unresolved events. Prioritize approaching deadlines, open calls, promising official-site leads and partial evidence.
8. Report confirmed-paid, payment-unresolved and unpaid/expenses-only separately. Prepare pitches only for cases whose audience, eligibility and route justify one. Closed calls can become future-edition watch cases, not open opportunities.

Do not call a complete contact case a confirmed booking. Revalidate dates, CFP status and terms before the client acts. Stop when the requested qualified batch is saved and checked, or report the exact shortfall and remaining research path.
