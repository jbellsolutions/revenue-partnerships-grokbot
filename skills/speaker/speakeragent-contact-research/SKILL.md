# Find the right contact

A name alone is not completion. An email beside an event is not proof it belongs to a named person. Read [contact evidence](references/evidence.md) and preserve the [case contract](../speakeragent/references/case-contract.md).

Start with the official event/show page, footer, speaker/guest application and named contact cards. Retain generic inboxes and organizer forms as useful routes. Keep `submission_form_url` and `contact_form_url` separate.

For unresolved cases choose the next missing-evidence strategy: confirm a known person's role; enrich an identified person if authorized; inspect official pages; establish organizer identity; inspect program/team/committee pages; use the browser for rendered pages; consult previous programs and organizer portfolios as leads. Do not repeat exhausted pages under new labels. Historical contacts need current confirmation.

Use up to eight research strategy steps per case as the source-derived default, with five primary pages per public-source step. This is an effort limit, not a financial cap. Reserve a browser step when rendering is needed. Record failure and retry counts; allow at most two transient public-read retries per strategy. Reconcile uncertain paid work rather than resubmitting it. Adjust the mission's limits only with the appropriate authorization.

For every candidate retain name, role, event relationship, email/LinkedIn or other supported professional route, URL, excerpt, observed date, current/historical status, source-checked status and channel ownership. Keep alternatives; never assemble one contact from different people's records. A provider can verify a channel, but cannot by itself prove responsibility for booking this event.

Declare `named_actionable` only when identity, current speaking-selection/enquiry role, event relationship and that person's channel are supported. A flagged identity stays manual-review. Declare `organizer_route` for a verified generic inbox or form without pretending it is a decision-maker. For podcasts, a host/producer/guest-booking route is relevant; an episode streaming page is not a booking form.

Stop once the required route is supported, or record source exhaustion, budget exhaustion, inaccessible source, person-without-channel or manual review. Save partial evidence and a concrete next move. A no-public-contact result is a legitimate outcome, not a reason to fabricate an email pattern.
