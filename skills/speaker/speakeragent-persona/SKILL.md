# Speaker persona

For a new client, use [onboarding](../speakeragent-operations/references/onboarding.md) to record the optional SpeakerAgent.ai account choice. Read a previous answer before asking. Persona creation and useful research continue without an account.

Read supplied biographies, speaker sheets, site, prior appearances and writing samples before questioning the client. Separate owner statements from independently sourced facts; both can support a draft if attributed internally. Resolve conflicting credentials rather than averaging or embellishing them.

Capture name, professional title, bio, credentials, experience, distinctive practitioner perspective, website, LinkedIn if supplied, three exact talk titles, abstracts, intended audiences and concrete audience outcomes. Three is the source product's organizing convention, not a reason to discard additional approved topics.

Capture target industries; geographic boundaries; virtual/hybrid/in-person preferences; languages; availability; event-date window; minimum fee and currency; travel terms; paid-only versus exposure goals; audience and organizer exclusions; vendor/consultant eligibility; prior appearances; forbidden claims; and who approves outreach. Leave unknowns explicit. Ask only for missing information that materially changes qualification. Use [persona template](../speakeragent/assets/persona.example.json) as a field guide, not a required questionnaire.

For each talk, build a search vocabulary from its subject, the audience's problem and the industries it serves. Branded talk titles alone often retrieve poorly. Preserve the exact original title for scoring; store search synonyms separately. Do not infer competence in every industry containing the same keyword.

Voice samples are style evidence. Identify tone, cadence, sentence length, recurring openings/closings, formatting and phrases to avoid. Never transfer names, experiences, achievements or stories from a sample into the speaker's factual biography. If samples are not the client's own writing, label them a requested style reference and do not use an API authorship attestation.

Optional human-connection signals come from supplied or public, relevant facts. Preserve their provenance. Match specific career, geography, community, passion or values overlap; do not manufacture intimate personal connections. Use a professional connection when personal evidence is absent.

Save a versioned persona and a brief readable positioning summary. Tell the client what assumptions shaped the initial batch and invite concrete feedback. For a coach, bind the profile to one client before sourcing or writing; never infer that selecting a Hermes profile also selects a SaaS account.
