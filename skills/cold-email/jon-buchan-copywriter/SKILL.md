# Jon Buchan copywriter (loader)

This skill works from licensed course material that can't be published, so the pack holds only this loader.

## Load the material

1. Look for the course folder, in this order: `$COURSE_MATERIAL_DIR/jon-buchan-copywriter/`, then
   `.secrets/course/jon-buchan-copywriter/` under the workspace. Justin gives it at intake.
2. If it's there, read its `SKILL.md` and follow it; its `references/` and `agents/` sit beside it.
3. If it isn't, say the course material is missing, and write in the plain house voice from
   `skills/cold-email/cold-email-master/references/buchan-techniques.md` (our own summary of the techniques) instead.

## House rules that always apply

- Justin's cold email voice is plain: no jokes or humour formulas unless he asks for them for one message.
- Every gate in GATES.md, the suppression check, and the do-not-mention list still apply.
- Never paste course text into a public file, a post, or this repo.
