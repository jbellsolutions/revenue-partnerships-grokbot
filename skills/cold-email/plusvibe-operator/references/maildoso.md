# Maildoso knowledge base (for the cold email specialist agent)

Compiled 2026-11-07 from Maildoso's own pages only. Citations: `[h:ID]` = https://intercom.help/maildoso/en/articles/ID (help centre, all 90 articles in the 9 collections read; ID-only URLs redirect to the slug page). `[b:name]` = https://blog.maildoso.ai/guides/name or /recommendations/name. Where I add my own view it is tagged "(my note)". Anything not found in the sources is listed under "Not documented". Maildoso is a vendor describing its own product; treat its claims as claims.

## Quick answers
- Aged or already-used mailboxes: no special reduced warmup is documented. Maildoso sells no pre-warmed mailboxes, only pre-warmed DOMAINS (beta). A mailbox that ran low-volume warmup elsewhere is treated as incomplete: set 80/day, 45% reply, ramp-up off, fresh 14 days [h:15435524].
- Plan lapse: failed payment = Past due, about 3 weeks grace, all works; then auto-cancel and mailboxes are deactivated (Inactive for a while, then removed). Cancelled-but-still-listed mailboxes can be revived via a NEW subscription from support with warmup history; once removed they are gone for good [h:15499509].
- Keep only some mailboxes: not self-serve; send support exact names; deleting does not lower the bill, the plan slots must be reduced [h:16554434].

## Warmup and sending limits
Maildoso does NOT run warmup. Warmup runs in a third-party tool (Instantly, Plusvibe, Email Bison), costs are billed by that tool; buying mailboxes does not start it [h:14166577, h:15435524]. The "AI Warmup Premium" add-on is closed to new subscribers; existing subscribers keep it [h:15435524]. API-enabled warmup only works with an active warmup subscription [h:14166577].
Per-mailbox numbers (Maildoso's "official" figures; they override sequencer defaults and sequencer AI advice) [h:14166577]:
- SMTP mailbox warmup: 80/day from day one, reply rate 45% (30-45% fine), slow ramp-up / increment per day OFF. Run 7 days a week. No staggering needed; upload all at once [h:14166577, h:15435524].
- SMTP campaign after 14-day warmup: max 15/day per mailbox including follow-ups. Optional slow ramp 5/day wk1, 10 wk2, 15 [h:14166577].
- Google Workspace (GW) mailbox: warmup 25/day, reply rate 30%, increment 2-3/day; campaign max 10/day after 14-day warmup and slow ramp-up [h:14166577].
- Tools that cap warmup at 50 (Plusvibe, EmailBison): set warmup 50, ramp-up off, campaign about 10/day. Email Bison can raise its cap to 100 on request; then keep 15/day. Two tools can split warmup (40+40); total per mailbox counts [h:14166577].
- Warmup stays on at 80/day ALONGSIDE campaigns; never switch it off. If you must change one number, lower campaign volume, not warmup. Most common cause of problems: low warmup plus high campaign volume [h:14166577, h:15435524].
- Hard cap: a mailbox is auto-deactivated above 100 total emails/day (warmup + campaign + follow-ups + replies). 80+15 = about 95 [h:14166577]. 80 is the ceiling for SMTP warmup; do not raise to make up lost days [h:15885318].
- Limits are per mailbox, not per domain: 20 mailboxes x 15 = 300/day. 20 to 50 SMTP mailboxes per domain recommended, up to 99 fine [h:14166577].
- The 14-day clock starts on the day the mailbox is set to the recommended settings (80/day) [h:15435524].
- Switching warmup tool: treat earlier low-volume warmup as incomplete; configure 80/45%/no ramp, plan a fresh 14 days before prospecting [h:15435524].
### Aged or pre-warmed mailboxes (what Maildoso says)
- Maildoso does not sell pre-made "already warmed" mailboxes at any volume. Every mailbox is delivered fresh [h:15435524].
- It offers pre-warmed DOMAINS (beta): domains warmed 3+ weeks in its network; mailboxes created on them can send campaigns on day one; you still turn warmup on in the sequencer and keep it running alongside campaigns [h:15435524, h:18296734].
- Nothing in the help centre gives a reduced warmup setting for aged or older mailboxes. The only exception documented is the pre-warmed domain case; for a mailbox whose earlier warmup was low-volume or in a different tool the guidance is to treat it as incomplete and run a fresh 14 days [h:15435524]. Mailboxes on ordinary new domains: about two weeks of warmup first [h:15435524].
### Low warmup alerts
- Alert is based on ACTUAL warmup emails sent/received, not the target. Seen on Home ("N mailboxes are sending fewer than 25 warmup emails per day"), weekly check-in and Insights > Mailbox volume [h:15885318].
- Causes: ramp-up on; target below 80 (sequencer default 20-50); warmup paused or mailbox disconnected; domain provisioned late; external tool throttle. Fix: ramp-up off, target 80 (or tool max), reconnect. Flagged mailboxes are usually at 10-40/day, not at a 50 cap [h:15885318, h:14166577].
- While warmup is below recommendation keep campaigns at or under 15/day (10 for tools capped at 50) [h:15885318].
### Warmup pool quality
- Biggest factor: share of Google Workspace mailboxes in the pool; above roughly 18% helps a lot. Instantly runs one of the stronger pools; Plusvibe adjusts Google share on request. Avoid pools sending identical subject/body for weeks [h:15435524].
- Smartlead is fine as sequencer, but its warmup pool is not recommended for SMTP mailboxes [h:14166577].
- Weak pool (Mailivery, Mailwarm) shows high spam rate with zero campaigns; switch to Instantly or Plusvibe at 50-80/day; recovery in a few days [h:15435524, h:15654687]. Some spam placement early in warmup is normal [h:15654687].
- Sequencer pauses warmup ("negatively impacting other users in the pool"): the tool's own safety rule, not a Maildoso flag. Reactivate via the code/link sent to that mailbox (not forwarded to Master Inbox; read over IMAP), then do not jump volume [h:16147706].
- Warmup Filters: set up in Master Inbox page > Set Up WarmUp filters (provider or custom warmup tag) so warmup is identified in Insights [h:14166577, h:14166602]. They do not apply to GW mailboxes (no Master Inbox); GW warmup mail lands in the forwarding address, so use a rule there or an unwatched address [h:16096360].
- A clean warmup does not guarantee safety: risky TLDs like .online can still face registry suspension [h:14166577].

## Deliverability rules, reputation, blocklists
- Content: first email plain text, no links; links ok in follow-ups. Open tracking OFF for whole sequence; click tracking on plain links acceptable. Minimal signature. Include a working unsubscribe line in the BODY: the relay strips List-Unsubscribe headers on every plan [h:14166572, h:16918330]. Rotate copy / spintax; small rewrites fix placement on long campaigns more often than infra changes [h:14166572].
- Keep bounce rate below 2%; verify lists; B2B only. Personal consumer addresses (gmail.com, yahoo, outlook.com etc.) can land in spam and risk suspension under the terms [h:14166572]. Mail to personal outlook.com/hotmail/live is not delivered at all (accepted, no bounce) [h:15586111].
-use external business addresses (GW or M365 on a non-Maildoso domain) or the sequencer placement test. Mail between Maildoso mailboxes is accepted but never delivered, no bounce [h:16231432].
- No bounce/DSN feed anywhere (dashboard, API, webhook); /v1/user/stats gives no recipient addresses or bounce reasons. Use SMTP send-time responses and the sequencer. Support can look up one message by sender, recipient, time, subject [h:16231432].
- Reputation scores: Email accounts page shows Google Score and Microsoft Score (High, Poor, Burned) plus last-check date; SMTP only (GW not tested). Check runs when triggered, not on a fixed schedule; support can trigger one. New mailboxes read low (even Burned) for about 2 weeks [h:15870067]. Below High after warmup: pause prospecting, run warmup-only 1-3 weeks; about 50% warmup / 50% outreach keeps reputation stable [h:15870067].
- Burned mailbox recovery: stop cold sends, keep warmup (SMTP 80/45%/no increment 1-2 weeks, up to 3 if severe; GW 25/30%/+2-3). Week 1 too early; week 2 look for gains; still poor week 3 = permanently burned; replace by delete and recreate in same slot at no charge, then warm [h:15849916].
- Sequencer "burned" label (Smartlead, Instantly) reflects that tool's pool, not Maildoso reputation: do not pause or replace on that alone [h:15849916].
- SURBL, SpamRATS, Spamhaus DBL listings of your domains: Maildoso says no practical effect on business-mailbox deliverability in 2026; do not delete, replace or file delisting; it does not file delistings; never qualifies for credit/refund/replacement. Google stopped using Spamhaus DBL Aug 2026 and SURBL a year before. Spamhaus lists batches of lookalike/branded domains; pick unique generic names [h:15268572, h:15849916]. (Maildoso's own position; my note: log listings but judge by real placement tests.)
- Audit tools: SPF softfail/mismatch vs smtp.maildoso.com, DKIM "missing" (own selector), generic DMARC, no PTR: expected. Prove auth with a real message: Gmail "Show original" Authentication-Results, or mail-tester.com. Custom DMARC policy/rua, own DKIM key, custom DNS records not configurable [h:16998208].
- Mailbox rotation (all accounts since mid-2025): first email of a thread may go out from another mailbox in your rotation group (grouped by redirect URL, Master Inbox forwarding address and mailbox names); follow-ups stay on the original mailbox; replies route back. Needs 2+ domains; set redirect URL on new domains; agencies: one group per client. Group shares one SMTP password while on. Can be turned off per account (all or nothing, minutes); then re-export CSV and reconnect, and burned mailboxes no longer self-rest [h:15443550].
- Domain TLDs: safe .com .info .co .one; avoid .online .site .xyz .space .website (registry suspensions); .sbs .cfd too risky; .click .top case by case; no double dash in names; extension does not change placement [h:16440088].
- Domain layout: 20-50 SMTP mailboxes per domain, up to 99; GW max 5; keep outreach domains apart from your main site domain [h:16440088].
- Missing MX / replies: verify at mxtoolbox first; domain "Active" but on parking nameservers (ns1.dyna-ns.net) needs support to re-delegate; do not edit nameservers yourself [h:15435713].

## Domains and DNS
- Maildoso manages all DNS (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Only action on your side: set exactly Maildoso's two nameservers at the registrar (no extras). It will not add/edit records (no A/CNAME, tracking CNAMEs, Postmaster TXT, custom DMARC/rua, MX) [h:15478052].
- Bring your own: Domains > Connect existing domains; CSV up to 100 domains; pick provider (SMTP or GW; stays linked); two nameservers per domain generated, set within 24h or they expire (Bulk actions > Regenerate NS); activates in about 2h, up to 24-48h propagation; disable DNSSEC first; Cloudflare Registrar domains cannot be connected [h:14166520, h:15990398, h:15478052]. BYO domains: no registration fee but use a domain slot; BYO not eligible for free replacement [h:14166520, h:14166564].
- Statuses: Pending, Processing, NS Not Set/Awaiting NS, NS Processing, NS Failed, NS Expired, Ready (do not send campaigns until Active), Active, Failed, DNS_ERROR, Awaiting Renewal, Expired, Inactive/Blocked (registry serverHold), Manually Disabled (.online/.xyz/.site suspensions). Rule: do not delete, re-add or re-buy to fix a stuck status; contact support [h:16431127].
- Domain replacement: $15 voluntary; free only for registry suspension, our-side expiry, undeliverable names, failed registration, or genuine delivery-blocking blacklist [h:14166564]. Transfer out: Dynadot push after ICANN 60-day lock; mailboxes are not transferred and stop sending; $15 to keep slot or free to drop it [h:16997794]. Moving mailboxes to another domain means delete and recreate with fresh warmup [h:16733834].
- GCDT: one global tracking domain, on by default (Settings > General); do not add your sequencer's tracking domain; SMTP only; can toggle off if links flagged [h:14166545].
- DKIM selector form smtpNNNNN. Instantly sending with no DKIM + SPF softfail = wrong SMTP host: delete mailbox in Instantly and re-add manually with smtp.maildoso.com:587 STARTTLS [h:15478052].
- Pre-warmed domains (beta): generic .com names, warmed 3+ weeks on a private pool, released only at 95%+ deliverability in Maildoso's tests. Buy a limit first (Packages & Add-ons), then choose domains from the catalog; up to 50 mailboxes (99 ok) and 750 cold/day per domain; SMTP only; mailboxes count toward the normal mailbox limit and need warmup running alongside campaigns; API supports list/activate/create-mailboxes, not the old reseller API [h:18296734].

## Mailboxes
- Types: SMTP (Maildoso) is the default; GW is hosted by Google via a third party reseller, max 5 per domain, new GW allotment not for sale; a domain serves one provider only and stays linked [h:15434858, h:16431131, h:16440088].
- Mailbox statuses: Active; Setup awaiting/processing (SMTP 30-90 min, escalate after 4-6h; GW hours, escalate after 24-48h); Inactive (cancelled sub, trial end, >100/day limit, voluntary pause; also briefly after connecting a sequencer); Failed (domain not ready, GW Admin deleted, GW address awaiting removal); Suspended - Domain expired; Suspended - Pre-warmed domain paused; Deletion scheduled - date [h:16202094, h:15434859]. Do not delete/recreate stuck mailboxes [h:15434859].
- Inactive is not deleted: reactivation keeps warmup history; then re-export CSV, reconnect, resume warmup before campaigns [h:16202094]. Voluntary pause: mailboxes deactivated, subscription payments paused, warmup history kept [h:16202094].
- Creating: Email accounts > Add more email accounts (3 steps: names, check, Register mailboxes); first+last name required (letters and dashes only); several domains per row; or Domains > Bulk actions > Create mailboxes; max 99 per domain [h:15551977, h:14166608].
- Names: SMTP display name changed by support without new warmup; alias cannot be edited for any type (new mailbox, 10-14 days warmup); GW renaming impossible [h:14166608]. Profile photos GW only (SMTP avatars would footprint) [h:15478062].
- Credentials: passwords only in the CSV export (email, password, IMAP host/port, SMTP host/port); not shown on screen; passwords can change after reactivation [h:16440105].
- Pool-level differences: domains sit in sending groups with differing reputation; cannot be moved; recovers via rotation; redirect URL + Master Inbox on every domain helps [h:15958511].
- Attachments are stripped from all SMTP mailboxes (campaigns, Master Inbox replies); link to a hosted file instead [h:16998214].
- GW specifics: slot not freed on delete until end of GW billing period; address cannot be recreated until then; never delete/cancel the Admin mailbox (cancels all GW on that domain, domain barred about 11 months); GW cannot be transferred in/out; GW forwarding to an external address via Bulk actions > Change forwarding for Google [h:16431131].

## Connection settings and sequencers (incl. PlusVibe)
- SMTP (all Maildoso SMTP mailboxes, every account): host smtp.maildoso.com, port 587 STARTTLS (465 SSL also works), username = full address, password from CSV. IMAP: host differs per mailbox, from the CSV (imap.<node>.maildoso.com, e.g. helicon, apollo, hector), port 993 SSL; IMAP hosts are never SMTP: pointing SMTP at one gives "250 OK queued" but silent non-delivery. Re-export the CSV; saved credentials go stale [h:16231432, h:16431129, h:15421658].
- Get credentials: Email accounts > Export accounts > Download selected/all accounts as CSV [h:14166556, h:16440105]. SMTP mailboxes are NOT supported by one-click sync in any sequencer ("Provider 'maildoso' is temporarily not supported"); use CSV [h:16431129].
- GW mailboxes: no CSV/SMTP login (535-5.7.8). Settings > Sequencers for GW lists Plusvibe, Emailbison, Instantly, ReachInbox, Warmy.io, Manyreach, Smartlead; then Email accounts > Provider=Google > Export accounts > Add selected Google accounts to Sequencer (about 1 hour, up to 24h; Latest Sequencer column Completed/Failed; blank for SMTP). Needs the sequencer login email and password (not API key), 2FA off [h:15885314, h:16431129].
- Only Active mailboxes on Active domains within plan limits can be imported; expired subscription or over-limit takes accounts out of Active [h:15830644].
- PlusVibe: Maildoso has no dedicated PlusVibe setup article. PlusVibe is named only in the troubleshooting list and in warmup guidance (warmup cap 50/day, so 50 warmup and about 10 campaign/day; good pool; will adjust Google share on request) [h:16431129, h:14166577, h:15435524]. Connect SMTP mailboxes by the CSV/IMAP-SMTP route above. Third-party tool steps beyond this are not documented by Maildoso.
- Instantly: Email Accounts > Add New > IMAP and SMTP Import (or CSV). Errors: 422 = IMAP validation failed at import (domain not Active/Awaiting Renewal/over plan limit/mailbox not Active/stale CSV: wait, export a fresh CSV, retry; do not add one by one or change SSL); 409 = already in a workspace, remove stale entry fully first. Mailbox can sit in one Instantly workspace only. Instantly "keep warmup below 50" warnings are generic: ignore. [h:15830644, h:16431129].
- Errors: 535 on every mailbox of one domain = domain not Active (NS Expired/Not Set/Inactive/suspended): fix the domain, then fresh CSV [h:16431129]. 535 after payment problem: support re-enables login [h:15564593]. Brand-new mailboxes failing with correct settings: known provisioning issue; contact support, do not recreate [h:16431129]. "Domain unavailable"/422 after expiry = domain suspended [h:16431129]. Mailbox vanishing from a sequencer warmup pool: known issue, report it [h:16431129]. IMAP refused/timeout/no banner: server-side, send support host and port [h:16733091]. Fully delete and re-add from fresh CSV instead of editing entries [h:15830644, h:16431129].

## Master Inbox, forwarding, onboarding
- Master Inbox: SMTP mailboxes only. Master Inbox page > Create Master Inbox, address on maildoso.email (free, no mailbox slot), password auto-generated; attach via Email accounts > Bulk actions > Change forwarding for Maildoso. SMTP mailboxes cannot forward to external addresses. No webmail: read over IMAP in BlueMail or Outlook (not Gmail). Master Inbox IMAP imap.apollo.maildoso.com:993 SSL, SMTP smtp.maildoso.com:587 STARTTLS [h:14166580, h:15690428].
- Not forwarded to Master Inbox: out-of-office/auto replies and OTP/verification codes (they stay in the individual mailbox; read via IMAP). Master Inbox often shows about 30% more replies than a sequencer [h:15690428, h:14166580].

## Billing, plan lapse, downsizing, cancelling
- Prices: SMTP mailboxes volume-priced on the whole account: 0-29 $2.80, 30-299 $2.50, 300-999 $0.75, 1000+ $0.50 per mailbox/month; min purchase 10; domains $15/domain/year (separate yearly charge, no discount); pre-warmed domains $11.99/domain/month (separate subscription). Monthly packages 30/$75, 300/$225, 1000/$499; legacy quarterly packages include domains [h:16770837, h:15551977, h:15579322]. No auto-repricing; consolidate via support [h:16919798]. New Google Workspace allotment is not for sale; existing GW stays [h:16770837].
- Billing via Stripe portal: Billing > Manage (card, invoices, details, cancel) [h:18297264]. Subscriptions cannot be bought or increased via API; API creates mailboxes only in already-bought slots [h:15579323]. API/MCP-created mailboxes beyond plan put the account Overlimit (no auto-charge, cannot create more until fixed) [h:17170897].
- Failed renewal: status Past due; grace period about three weeks, everything keeps working while card is retried; pay from Billing > Manage anytime in the window. Still unpaid then: subscription auto-cancelled and mailboxes deactivated (listed as Inactive for a while, then removed). Ask support before the grace ends for a short hold [h:15499509, h:15564593].
- Case 2 (cancelled, mailboxes still listed as Inactive): do NOT pay the old invoice; support creates a NEW subscription for the same mailboxes via payment link and reactivates them with warmup history and reputation [h:15499509].
- Case 3 (cancelled and paid period ended, mailboxes gone from list): they are removed and cannot be restored by anyone or by a new plan; a new plan gives fresh slots, new mailboxes on existing domains, full 14-day warmup [h:15499509].
- After a past-due payment an Inactive/535-login mailbox may stay blocked until support re-enables it; then re-export CSV and reconnect [h:15499509, h:15564593].
- Plan lapse and domains: domains renew only if subscription is active on the domain renewal date; a gap means Awaiting Renewal/Expired; expired domain stops resolving, mailboxes show "Suspended - Domain expired", sequencers show 422. Registry grace may allow renewal after subscription returns; else a replacement domain in the slot [h:15499509, h:16431127]. Cancelled subscription = Maildoso stops managing domain renewals [h:14166564].
- Voluntary pause on request: mailboxes deactivated, payments paused, warmup history kept; account pause is one month [h:16202094, h:15421654].
- Pre-warmed domain unpaid: 0-7 days Past due works; after 7 days newest domains above the paid limit pause (mailboxes "Suspended - Pre-warmed domain paused"); pay or delete to resume; after 7 more days paused, domains removed and mailboxes deleted. They are rented and cannot be transferred out [h:18296734].
- Downsizing or reducing a plan can auto-remove mailboxes; you cannot choose which. If you have fewer mailboxes than the new limit nothing is removed, so do not delete pre-emptively; ask support. Do not cancel the old plan before the new one is active (slots are removed when it ends) [h:16554434, h:16919798].
- Cancelling self-serve: Billing > Manage > Cancel subscription per subscription; access until period end; status shows Canceled. Cancelled SMTP mailboxes show "Deletion scheduled - date" and stay working until then, then slot frees. Deleting a mailbox does not lower the bill; the plan slots must be reduced via support (applies next billing cycle) [h:15421654, h:16554434].
- Keep only some mailboxes/domains: partial changes are not self-serve; send support the exact names to keep and remove. Domain removal with plan reduction is free (about 10-15 min); removal keeping the slot costs $15/domain; no refund on the unused registration [h:16554434, h:15421654]. Subscription cancel does not auto-remove domains; tell support which [h:16554434].
- Refund: only within 30 days of first payment ever, and to qualify SMTP mailboxes must run 80/day warmup for 14 days with ramp-up off; domain cost ($15) and time used deducted; GW non-refundable; refunds net of processor fee, 5-10 business days; re-subscribing may earn one free month credit [h:15421654].
- Delete is one-way: mailbox/domain and warmup history cannot be restored; recreating same address restarts warmup; recreating may show "already exists" for a while. Inactive is not deleted. Deleting a domain deletes all its mailboxes. Never delete the GW Admin mailbox (cancels all GW on that domain, domain unusable for GW about 11 months) [h:16554434].

## Conflicts between Maildoso sources (help centre wins; it says "where a video and a Help Center article differ, the article is authoritative" [h:15566085])
- Unsubscribe header: blog guide tells you to insert the unsubscribe header in Instantly/Smartlead [b:deliverability-guide]; help centre says the relay strips List-Unsubscribe on every plan, so use a body line [h:16918330].
- Mailboxes per domain: blog says 5 per domain [b:maildoso-what-it-is-and-how-to-get-started]; help centre says 20-50, up to 99 SMTP, GW max 5 [h:16440088].
- Links in first email: help centre "plain text, no links" [h:14166572]; blog says any link is fine in a first email thanks to GCDT link replacement [b:deliverability-guide]. (my note: follow the stricter help centre rule for first touches.)
- Mailbox recovery: blog describes 14 days sending / 14 days rest cycles [b:maildoso-what-it-is-and-how-to-get-started]; help centre describes rotation and a 14-day pause of burned mailboxes [h:16499185, h:15443550].
