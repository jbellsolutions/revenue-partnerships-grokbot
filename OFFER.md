# The offer: what this bot sells, and the words for it

Every role reads this page before it drafts. It is the source for what is sold; the rest of the pack is how the work is done.

## In one line

Justin, 2026-11-18: "We help small businesses and solopreneurs grow their business through outreach, partnerships, sponsors, affiliates, podcasts, and media opportunities."

## The message

His words, 2026-11-18. Copy uses these, in his plain voice:

- "Never buy leads again."
- "Never pay for leads again."
- "Stop paying for marketing."
- "Turn your partnerships or relationships into true revenue partnerships. That's the main point there."
- Why now, as he put it: paid ads, "everything's crazy"; people "can't trust content, what you see"; "people are only going to believe trusted sources."

The stance is against the established way of buying growth: against paid ads and bought leads, for trusted sources (partners, hosts, people an audience already believes).

## Given at intake, never in this pack

- The percentage, and what counts as revenue that comes from a partnership.
- The call link every lane ends on.
- Whether outreach itself names the prices, or they are said only on a reply and on the call. Until Justin says, a first touch names no price and the sales agent's answer to "what does it cost" is a draft for him.
- The guarantee. Justin, 2026-11-18: "I guarantee you, I'll get you at least 5 new partners in 90 days, or you don't pay." How that sits beside $2,000 up front (refunded on a miss, or paid after) is his to say, so no message states the guarantee until he does.

## Who it is for

Small businesses and solopreneurs. A good fit already has something a partner would want to be near: a list, an audience, a referral network, past customers, or an offer other people's customers need.

## Proof the bot may point at

Only what Justin gives at intake, in his words, with first names only. Nothing is invented: no numbers, results or client names that are not on that list.
