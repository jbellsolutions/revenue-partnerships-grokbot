# Trigger map

The loop is deterministic: schedules and webhooks start every step, and the agent makes only the judgment call inside it. Gates stay on until replies prove good. Every cron is UTC (set the bot's time zone to UTC). **50 routines max per bot, schedules and webhook routines together**; `tools/check.py` counts both. This section's routine tables are generated by `tools/gen_roles.py`.

## The loop, step by step

| # | Step | Started by | Role | Judgment call | Human gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan the day | Schedule `10 12 * * *` | lead | What matters today, which role is behind | None |
| 2 | Build and repair scrapers | Schedule `10 4 * * *`, and any `requests/cold-email/scraper-*.md` | cold-email | Which rung, which fields, is the output right | Paid rungs past $5 a day: Justin |
| 3 | Find | The worker: Skool read and score, radar poll, show research every 2 hours, list builds and scrapers (WORKERS.md). Bot schedules: lists and orders 12:35; LinkedIn 12:45; partner find 11:50; collab blitz 18:20; show shortlist 14:30; dossiers 16:20; creators 17:10 | social, cold-email, partners, speaker, dream | Is this a real fit (asker, show, partner)? | None: research only |
| 4 | Check | Inside each run (`suppression.py`) | the same | None: code decides | None |
| 5 | Draft first touch | Same run | cold-email, social, partners, speaker, channels, capital, sales-crm (openers) | Copy | Every new message type: Justin approves once |
| 6 | Send or post | The sending tool's schedule (PlusVibe, Instantly), the guarded sender, or the browser | cold-email, sales-crm, speaker, social, partners, channels | None: `send_guard.py` decides | Ask-first until replies prove good; each channel's first post |
| 7 | Reply arrives | The worker pulls and sorts every reply every 15 minutes, takes opt-outs at once, and wakes the **Reply webhook** routine with hot ones; the Reply check reads the digest three times a day as the backstop | sales-crm | Reply sort and draft | Every reply send ask-first at the start |
| 8 | Record | Same run | sales-crm | Next step and date | GoHighLevel writes off until `crm-writes: on` |
| 9 | Book | **Event:** Booking webhook (Cal.com) | sales-crm | Call brief | None (no message to a person) |
| 10 | Get cited | Schedules: AI visibility read Mon 14:45, SEO daily 15:25, article pick Tue and Thu 16:10, links Fri 16:10, Medium Tue and Thu 19:05, channel read Fri 17:55 | content-seo | Which gap to close, which source earns a citation | Paid RankSpot actions; site changes merge only by Justin |
| 11 | Channels | Schedules: X 14:25 and 19:45, YouTube briefs Mon and Thu 18:25; daily content 22:05 (content engine) | channels, content-seo | Topic, hook, which reply is worth it | First post per channel; each type ask-first; X and LinkedIn posted by Justin by hand, never by the bot's browser |
| 12 | Follow up and close loops | Pipeline sweep 13:25, 17:25, 21:25; partner tickets every 3 hours; daily close 23:20 | sales-crm, partners | Stage moves on evidence | Follow-up sends ask-first |
| 13 | Report | The worker's watchdog every 30 minutes (no model); evening scoreboard 23:40 | lead | Proof check on each number | None |

## Every schedule and event, by role

39 schedules and 2 event routines: 41 of the 50 a bot allows, leaving 9 spare. No two start within 5 minutes of each other (checked by `tools/gen_roles.py`). Chat requests from Justin are not routines. Scheduled bot runs with every function on: 272 a week; the volume work runs on the worker (WORKERS.md). The Function column is the Revenue OS function a routine belongs to (`core` runs on every tier); `python3 tools/tier.py <tier>` lists the routines a tier creates.
